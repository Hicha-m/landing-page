# Code of Conduct

We want StudioSpace to be a welcoming project for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Focus criticism on ideas and code, not individuals. Do not engage in harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language.

## Reporting

If you encounter harmful behavior, contact the maintainer privately rather than posting personal information publicly. Reports will be reviewed with care and discretion.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation to keep discussions productive.
